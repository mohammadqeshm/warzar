# WARZAR: Crate Rush · Audio credits (1.0.0)

The 88 recorded and produced licensed MP3s are shared by all builds. The previous code-rendered WARZAR score and cues remain in the source tree for reference only; no current build packages or plays them. The runtime only decodes and mixes compressed samples. No audio is fetched from a third-party origin.

**Changes:** WAV/OGG/FLAC excerpts were trimmed, leveled and MP3 encoded. The motor recording was converted to mono, seam-blended and EQ'd in six matching bands for the in-game RPM mix. The wind was seam-blended, EQ'd and panned at runtime. The menu track has a short seam blend; on a mission the game crossfades to the separate music loop. Short effects were trimmed, pitch-adjusted where indicated, and faded. The audio manifest records the source filename and exact MP3 boundaries for every cut. The author links above are attribution, not a claim of endorsement or catalog acceptance. These credits ship in the upload ZIP.
